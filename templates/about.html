Our Story

Where Every Day Feels Like a Holiday

Born in 2014 from a simple dream — to create a place where families could escape, play and reconnect — AquaVerse has grown into Sunshine Bay's most-loved water park and resort.

Today we welcome over half a million visitors each year across 25+ attractions, eight pools and a world-class events calendar, all wrapped in award-winning safety and hygiene standards.

🎯

Our Mission

To deliver safe, joyful, world-class aquatic experiences that bring families and communities together — one splash at a time.

🔭

Our Vision

To be the most trusted and loved family water destination, setting the global benchmark for fun, safety and sustainability.

Cleanliness & Maintenance
